# Team Communication Norms
**Channel & Tool Usage**
We will be communicating via Instagram Group Chat and Email.

**Response Times**
Team members must respond to messages and notifications within 24 hours.

**Meeting Etiquette**
Meetings are held on Mondays and Wednesdays. The meetings consist of weekly reminders and progress checks.

**Pull Request Tone**
Pull Requests must be written formally and in standard format.
